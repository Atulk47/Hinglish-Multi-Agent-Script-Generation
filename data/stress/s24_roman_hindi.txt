Pehla paragraph: Ravi aur Sameer, dono chhote se gaon ke chhote chhote cousins, dono ek saath mela mein gaye. Gaon ke mela mein sab kuch alag hi tha – rang-birange flags, dhoom-dhaam, aur logon ki khushboo. Ravi, jo thoda sharmila tha, Sameer se bola, “Yeh sab dekh ke thoda darr lag raha hai.” Sameer ne muskura kar kaha, “Chinta mat kar, main hoon na.”

Doosra paragraph: Dono ne ek dum se ek chhota sa stall dekha jahan se rang-birange laddus aur jalebi milti thi. Ravi ne laddus khane ka idea rakha aur Sameer ne jalebi. Ek dum se stall ke owner ne unko ek naya challenge diya – “Sabse tezi se laddus khane ka competition.” Ravi ne socha, “Yeh toh mazedaar hoga!” Sameer ne bhi haan kar diya.

Tisra paragraph: Competition start hua aur dono ne ek ek laddus khana shuru kiya. Ravi ne pehla laddus khaya aur phir Sameer ne. Dono ne apne aap ko challenge kiya, lekin jalebi ka taste thoda mushkil lag raha tha. Sameer ne ek choti si ghalti ki aur jalebi ka ek tukda hi khatam kar diya. Ravi ne bhi ek tukda khatam kar diya. Dono ne hasi mazaak karte competition ko jari rakha.

Chautha paragraph: Competition ke beech mein, gaon ke log unko dekhte rahe aur khush hote gaye. Ek chhota sa bachcha, jo dono ka chhota bhai tha, unko dekhkar bolta, “Bhai, aap dono ko kaise pata chala ki laddus aur jalebi ka taste alag hai?” Ravi ne jawab diya, “Yehi toh humara secret recipe hai.” Sameer ne bhi khushi se kaha, “Haan, aur hum dono ko ek dum se ek aur challenge milega.”

Paanchwa paragraph: Competition ke end mein, dono ne ek dusre ko high-five kiya, aur gaon ke logon ne unko congratulation diya. Ravi ne kaha, “Ab hum dono ke beech mein ek nayi friendship ban gayi hai.” Sameer ne bhi haan kiya, “Aur hum aage bhi ek dusre ke saath is tarah ke fun competitions karega.” Dono ne ek saath chhote se gaon ke mela ko enjoy kiya, aur ek naya yaadgar din ban gaya.
