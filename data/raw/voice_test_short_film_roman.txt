Aditya, Neha, Karan aur Simran ek hi engineering college mein padhte the aur chaaron pakke dost the. Ek din college mein saalana festival Utsav ke short film competition ki ghoshna hui. Jeetne wali team ko pachchis hazaar rupaye aur poore college ke saamne apni film dikhane ka mauka milne wala tha. Aditya ne canteen mein sabko bulaya aur josh mein bola, "Yaar, is baar apni film banate hain. Mere dimaag mein ek kamaal ki kahani hai." Neha ne hanskar kaha, "Pehle kahani sunao, phir decide karenge." Simran ne chai ka cup uthate hue kaha, "Editing meri zimmedari, bas script mast honi chahiye."

Agle teen din tak Aditya aur Neha ne library mein baithkar script likhi. Kahani ek ladke ki thi jo apne gaon se shehar padhne aata hai aur dheere dheere apni pehchaan khone lagta hai. Lekin ending par dono mein behes chhid gayi. Neha chahti thi ki film khushi par khatam ho, jabki Aditya ko udaas ending pasand thi. Neha ne gusse mein kaha, "Tum hamesha apni hi baat kyun manwate ho?" Aditya ne chidhkar jawab diya, "Kyunki ye meri kahani hai!" Aadhe ghante tak dono ne ek doosre se baat nahi ki, aur Karan aur Simran bechaare beech mein phans gaye.

Usi shaam Karan ne dono ko hostel ki chhat par bulaya aur samjhaya, "Yaar, ladna band karo. Hum film bana rahe hain, ek doosre ko haraane ke liye nahi." Simran ne bhi kaha, "Ending ke do versions shoot kar lete hain, phir editing mein dekhenge." Aditya ko apni galti ka ehsaas hua aur usne Neha se maafi maangi. Neha muskurayi aur boli, "Theek hai, par agli baar meri baat bhi sunna." Dono hans pade, aur chaaron ne milkar shooting ka plan banaya.

Shanivaar ki subah chaaron college ke peeche wale purane bagiche mein shooting karne pahunche. Lekin wahan guard ne unhe rok diya aur kaha ki bina permission ke yahan film nahi ban sakti. Aditya ne bahut manaya, par guard nahi maana. Karan ko bahut bura laga, aur usne pareshaan hokar kaha, "Ab kya karein? Itni mushkil se plan banaya tha." Tabhi Neha ko yaad aaya ki Professor Mishra drama club ke incharge hain. Wo bhaagi bhaagi unke paas gayi. Professor Mishra ne poori baat suni aur kaha, "Mehnat karne walon ko main kabhi nahi rokta. Ye lo likhit anumati." Chaaron ne unhe dil se shukriya kaha.

Shooting do din chali. Karan camera sambhal raha tha aur Aditya hero ka kirdaar nibha raha tha. Doosre din dopahar mein achanak camera ka memory card kharab ho gaya aur aadha footage ud gaya. Karan ke chehre ka rang ud gaya. Usne ghabrakar kaha, "Mujhse badi galti ho gayi. Sab khatam ho gaya." Simran ne uska haath pakadkar kaha, "Pagal, bas ek hi shoot dobara karna hai, duniya khatam nahi hui." Neha ne bhi kaha, "Hum phir se kar lenge, bas hausla mat chhodna."

Us raat Karan ne apne bade bhaiya Rohit ko phone kiya, jo shehar mein video editing ka kaam karte the. Rohit hostel aaye, card ko check kiya aur bole, "Tension mat lo, thoda footage wapas aa sakta hai." Hostel ke warden Sharma sir ne unhe raat bhar common room mein baithne ki ijazat de di. Chaaron raat bhar jaagte rahe. Subah chaar baje Rohit ne muskurakar bataya ki zyadatar footage wapas mil gaya hai. Simran khushi se uchhal padi aur boli, "Bhaiya, aap to ekdum hero nikle!"

Film jama karne se pehle ke teen din Simran ne editing mein jaan daal di. Usne dono endings ko ek hi film mein jod diya, jisme pehle udaas ending thi aur uske baad ek chhota sa khushnuma epilogue. Jab chaaron ne film dekhi to Neha ki aankhon mein aansoo aa gaye. Aditya ne dheere se kaha, "Ye to meri soch se bhi achhi bani hai." Karan ne sabko gale lagaya aur kaha, "Ab bas kismat ka intezaar hai."

Utsav ke din college ke auditorium mein hazaaron students jama the. Jab chaaron ki film chali to poora hall shaant ho gaya, aur ant mein zor ki taaliyan gunj uthin. Parinaam ki ghoshna mein unki team ka naam pehle number par aaya. Neha khushi se cheekh padi, "Hum jeet gaye! Mujhe yakeen nahi ho raha!" Aditya ne hanskar kaha, "Maine kaha tha na, ye kahani kamaal hai." Shaam ko chaaron ne Professor Mishra, Sharma sir aur Rohit bhaiya ko cake khilakar shukriya kaha, aur wo pal unhe hamesha yaad raha.
