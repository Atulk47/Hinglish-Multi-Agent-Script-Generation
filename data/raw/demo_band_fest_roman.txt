Somvaar dopahar ko college canteen mein bahut shor tha, lekin sabse zyada shor ek hi table par tha. Aarav, Simran, Kabir aur Meera ek saath baithe the, aur Aarav ne abhi abhi notice board se photo kheenchi thi: "Utsav" ke inter-college band battle ki registration khul gayi thi. Aarav khushi ke maare kursi se uchhal pada. "Dekho dekho, agle Shanivaar ko hai, humein hi toh jeetna hai!" Simran ne poster padha aur uski aankhen chamak gayi. Use yakeen nahi ho raha tha ki unka sapna itni jaldi saamne aa gaya. Kabir ne muskurate hue apni guitar ke cover par haath phera, aur Meera ne turant sabka naam form mein likhna shuru kar diya. Unhone apne band ka naam rakha "Sunset Four". Chaaron ko lag raha tha ki ab sab kuch badal jayega.

Shaam ko college ke music room mein pehli practice thi. Simran sabse pehle pahunch gayi thi, aur woh bahut der se ghadi dekh rahi thi. Kabir poore chalis minute late aaya. Aate hi uski guitar ki ek taar toot gayi, aur woh jhunjhla gaya. Simran ka sabr jawab de gaya. Usne kaha ki yeh koi mazaak nahi hai, sab log mehnat kar rahe hain aur Kabir ko bas apni hi parwah hai. Kabir ko yeh baat chubh gayi aur usne bhi ulta jawab de diya. Kamre ka mahaul ekdum kharab ho gaya. Aarav dono ke beech aakar bola ki bas karo, lekin koi sunne ko taiyaar nahi tha. Meera chupchaap ek kone mein khadi rahi, use bahut bura lag raha tha. Practice us din adhoori hi chhoot gayi.

Raat ko hostel ki chhat par Kabir akela baitha tha. Meera chai ke do cup lekar uske paas aayi. Kabir kaafi der tak kuch nahi bola. Phir usne dheere se bataya ki ghar par papa ko uska music bilkul pasand nahi hai, woh chahte hain ki woh sirf padhai kare. Isi tension mein woh subah se pareshaan tha, aur isiliye late ho gaya. Meera ne uska kandha thapthapaya aur kaha ki woh akela nahi hai. Kabir ki aankhon mein nami thi, par uske chehre par halki si muskaan bhi aa gayi. Use pehli baar laga ki doston ke saath sab sambhal jayega.

Agle din dopahar mein Kabir sabse pehle music room pahuncha, nayi taar ke saath. Usne Simran se seedhe maafi maangi aur Simran ne bhi hanste hue maan liya ki woh zyada gussa ho gayi thi. Phir un chaaron ne apna gaana shuru kiya, aur is baar sab kuch jaise apne aap jud gaya. Aarav ke drums, Meera ki aawaz, Simran ka bass, sab ek taal mein the. Gaana khatam hua toh kuch der koi nahi bola, phir sab ek saath chillane lage. Simran ne kaha ki aaj tak ka sabse zabardast practice session tha. Chaaron ko apne upar naaz ho raha tha.

Shukravaar shaam ko auditorium mein sound check tha. Sab kuch theek chal raha tha ki achanak Aarav ka laptop band ho gaya, aur usi mein unka poora backing track tha. Aarav ka chehra safed pad gaya. Woh baar baar laptop ko restart karta raha, lekin kuch nahi hua. Simran ghabra gayi, Kabir ne apna sir pakad liya, aur Meera ko samajh nahi aaya ki kya kare. Tabhi wahan khadi ek senior, Tanvi, jo sound ka kaam sambhal rahi thi, ne bataya ki woh kal raat ki recording ka ek purana backup nikal sakti hai. Sabki jaan mein jaan aayi. Aarav ne dil se uska shukriya ada kiya.

Shanivaar ki shaam ko stage ke peeche sab bahut ghabraye hue the. Meera ke haath kaanp rahe the, aur woh baar baar apna gala saaf kar rahi thi. Simran ne uska haath pakad kar kaha ki ek lamba saans le. Kabir ne mazaak kiya ki agar woh bhool bhi gayi toh woh sab sambhal lega. Sab hans pade, aur ghabrahat thodi kam hui. Chaaron ne saath mein haath ek ke upar ek rakha. Bahar se ghoshna hui, "Ab aapke saamne, Sunset Four!" aur unka dil zor se dhadakne laga.

Stage par roshni padte hi sab kuch badal gaya. Aarav ne drumsticks uthayi, Kabir ki guitar goonji, aur Meera ki aawaz poore hall mein phail gayi. Neeche baithe doston ne chillana shuru kar diya. Gaane ke beech mein Simran ne Kabir ki taraf dekha aur dono ek saath muskuraye. Janta tali bajane lagi, kuch log toh khade ho gaye. Aakhri note par poora auditorium gunj utha. Chaaron ki aankhon mein khushi ke aansoo the. Unhe laga jaise unhone duniya jeet li ho.

Raat ko result aaya. Sunset Four doosre number par aaye, pehla inaam kisi aur ko mila. Ek pal ke liye Aarav ka chehra utar gaya, aur Simran ne gusse mein muththi bheench li. Lekin phir Meera hans padi, aur dheere dheere sab hanste hue lot-pot ho gaye. Woh chaaron wapas canteen pahunche aur raat ke gyarah baje chai peete hue agle saal ki planning karne lage. Kabir ne kaha ki is baar toh unhone sirf trailer dikhaya tha. Aarav ne kaha ki agle saal pehla inaam unka hi hoga. Un chaaron ke liye woh raat sabse yaadgaar thi.
