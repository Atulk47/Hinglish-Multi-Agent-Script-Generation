Mai 1983 ki ek garam subah, Mumbai ke airport par Indian cricket team England jaane ke liye taiyaar thi. Captain Kapil Dev ke saath Sunil Gavaskar, Mohinder Amarnath, Kris Srikkanth aur baaki khiladi khade the. Kisi ko umeed nahi thi ki ye team kuch karegi. London ke sattebaaz India par chhiyasath ke badle ek ka bhaav laga rahe the, yaani jeetne ki umeed lagbhag zero. Ek patrakaar ne Kapil se poocha, "Kya aap sach mein World Cup jeetne aaye hain?" Kapil muskuraye aur bole, "Hum khelne aaye hain, aur poori jaan lagakar khelenge."

Nau June ko Manchester ke Old Trafford maidan par pehla match West Indies ke khilaaf tha, jo do baar ki World Cup champion thi. Sab ko lag raha tha ki India haar jayega. Lekin Yashpal Sharma ki shaandaar pari aur gendbaazon ki tight bowling ne kamaal kar diya, aur India ne chauntees run se jeet darj ki. Dressing room mein khushi ka mahaul tha. Roger Binny ne hanste hue kaha, "Pehla match jeet gaye, ab to hawa badal gayi!"

Athaarah June ko Tunbridge Wells mein Zimbabwe ke saamne India ki shuruaat bahut buri thi. Sirf satrah run par paanch wicket gir chuke the. Dressing room mein sannata chha gaya. Tabhi Kapil Dev balla lekar maidan mein utre. Unhone pehle gend sambhalkar khela, phir chauke-chhakke barsane shuru kar diye. Kapil ne ek sau pachhattar run naabad banaye. Afsos ki us din BBC ki hadtaal ki wajah se is pari ka koi TV prasaaran nahi hua. Match ke baad Syed Kirmani ne kaha, "Kapil, aaj tumne India ko bacha liya!"

Baees June ko semi-final mein India ka saamna host England se tha, aur Old Trafford phir se gawah bana. Sandeep Patil aur Yashpal Sharma ki balle baazi ke aage England ki gendbaazi bikhar gayi, aur India ne chhe wicket se jeet hasil ki. Ye pehli baar tha jab India World Cup ke final mein pahuncha. Sandeep Patil ne hanskar kaha, "Ab Lord's mein bhi yahi kar ke dikhayenge!"

Pachchis June ki subah Lord's ke dressing room mein tanaav tha. Saamne do baar ki champion West Indies thi, jisme Viv Richards aur Gordon Greenidge jaise khatarnak ballebaaz the. Kapil Dev ne sabko ek jagah bulaya aur kaha, "Darne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Yahan tak aa gaye hain, ab bas apna sab kuch laga do." Gavaskar ne sar hilaya aur kaha, "Chalo, itihaas banane ka waqt hai."

Dopahar tak India ki batting khatam ho gayi, aur poori team sirf ek sau tirasi run par out ho gayi. Zyaadatar logon ne maan liya ki West Indies ye lakshya aasaani se hasil kar legi. Dressing room mein sab chup the. Tab Kapil ne kaha, "Abhi match baaki hai. Ek ek wicket ke liye ladna hai." Kris Srikkanth ne paani ki bottle uthate hue kaha, "Kuch bhi ho jaye, hum aakhri gend tak ladenge."

Shaam ko jab West Indies ki pari shuru hui, Balwinder Sandhu ne Gordon Greenidge ko clean bold kar diya. Greenidge ne gend ko chhod diya tha, par wo andar aakar stumps se jaa lagi. Phir Viv Richards ne chauke-chhakke lagakar India ko darra diya. Tabhi Madan Lal ki gend par Richards ne hawa mein shot khela, aur Kapil Dev peeche ki taraf bhaage aur gend ko catch kar liya. Stadium mein jaise bijli daud gayi. Madan Lal aur Mohinder Amarnath ek ke baad ek wicket nikaalte gaye, aur West Indies ek sau chaalis run par out ho gayi.

India ne ye final taintalis run se jeet liya. Lord's ki balcony par Kapil Dev ne World Cup trophy uthayi, aur poore desh mein jashn shuru ho gaya. Mohinder Amarnath ko Man of the Match chuna gaya. Baad mein jab BCCI ke paas khiladiyon ko dene ke liye paise kam pade, to Lata Mangeshkar ne Delhi mein concert karke paise jutaye, aur har khiladi ko ek lakh rupaye mile. Us din ke baad India mein cricket sirf ek khel nahi, ek junoon ban gaya.
