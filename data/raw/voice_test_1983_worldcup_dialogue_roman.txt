Mai 1983 ki ek garam subah, Mumbai ke airport par Indian cricket team England jaane ke liye khadi thi. Srikkanth ne Kapil Dev se kaha, "Skipper, London ke sattebaaz hamare upar chhiyasath ke badle ek ka bhaav laga rahe hain. Matlab koi humein jeetta hua dekhta hi nahi!" Kapil hans pade, "Achha hai na, ab koi dabaav nahi. Hum bas maze se khelenge." Sunil Gavaskar ne muskurakar kaha, "Maze ke saath thodi jeet bhi le aana, Kapil." Kapil ne sar hilaya, "Pakka, Sunny. Poori jaan laga denge."

Nau June ki shaam Manchester ke Old Trafford ke dressing room mein khushi ka shor tha. India ne do baar ki champion West Indies ko chauntees run se haraya tha. Roger Binny ne chillakar kaha, "Yaar, humne West Indies ko haraya hai! Mujhe abhi bhi yakeen nahi ho raha!" Yashpal Sharma ne hanskar jawab diya, "Mujhe bhi nahi. Bas bat chal gaya." Syed Kirmani bole, "Ab dekhna, log humein mazaak mein nahi lenge."

Athaarah June ko Tunbridge Wells mein Zimbabwe ke saamne India ke satrah run par paanch wicket gir gaye. Dressing room mein sab ghabraye hue the. Gavaskar ne dheere se kaha, "Ye to bahut buri shuruaat hai." Tabhi Kapil Dev balla lekar maidan mein gaye aur ek sau pachhattar run naabad bana liye. Baad mein Balwinder Sandhu ne hairaan hokar poocha, "Kapil, ye tumne kya kar diya?" Kapil muskuraye, "Bas gend dekhi aur maar di." Afsos ki us din BBC ki hadtaal ki wajah se is pari ka TV par prasaaran nahi hua.

Baees June ko semi-final jeetne ke baad bus mein sab khushi se jhoom rahe the. Madan Lal ne zor se kaha, "Hum final mein hain! Maa ko phone karna padega!" Mohinder Amarnath ne usse samjhaya, "Pehle final jeet to lo, phir jashn manana." Sandeep Patil ne mazaak kiya, "Tum dono ladna band karo, abhi to Lord's baaki hai!" Poori bus hansi se gunj uthi.

Chaubees June ki raat London ke hotel mein kisi ko neend nahi aa rahi thi. Sandhu ne Kapil se kaha, "Skipper, mere pet mein titliyan udh rahi hain. Kal Viv Richards ko kaise rokenge?" Kapil ne unke kandhe par haath rakhkar kaha, "Ek ek gend ki baat hai, Balwinder. Darne ki zaroorat nahi, bas apna kaam karo." Kirmani ne chai ka cup thamate hue kaha, "Sab so jao, kal ki fikar kal."

Pachchis June ko Lord's par India sirf ek sau tirasi run par out ho gayi. Dressing room mein sannata tha. Srikkanth ne udaas hokar kaha, "Itne run mein kaun bachayega?" Kapil ne sabko dekhkar kaha, "Abhi match baaki hai. Ek ek wicket ke liye ladna hai." Gavaskar ne bhi saath diya, "Kapil sahi keh raha hai. Hum aakhri gend tak ladenge."

Shaam ko Sandhu ki ek gend par Gordon Greenidge ne ball chhodi, aur wo andar aakar stumps se ja lagi. Kirmani ne wicket ke peeche se chillakar kaha, "Clean bold! Maza aa gaya!" Phir Viv Richards ne chauke-chhakke lagaye, tabhi Madan Lal ki gend par usne hawa mein shot khela, aur Kapil peeche ki taraf bhaag kar catch pakad liya. Binny ne chillakar poocha, "Kapil, ye catch tumne kaise pakda?" Kapil hanse, "Pata nahi, bas daudta gaya."

Raat ko Lord's ki balcony par Kapil Dev ne trophy uthayi. India ne West Indies ko ek sau chaalis run par out karke taintalis run se final jeet liya tha. Amarnath ko Man of the Match mila. Amarnath ne hanskar kaha, "Maine to bas apna kaam kiya, asli hero Kapil hai." Baad mein BCCI ke paas khiladiyon ko dene ke liye paise kam pade, to Lata Mangeshkar ne Delhi mein concert karke paise jutaye, aur har khiladi ko ek lakh rupaye mile.
